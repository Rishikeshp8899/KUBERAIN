Total Audits: 7 Specialized Tests
Pressure Audit

Hydrostatic Pressure & Pneumatic Leak Test

Evaluates structural integrity, shell tightness, and seat leakage under extreme fluid pressure to prevent field failures. Conducted on all industrial valves, pressure vessels, and forged high-pressure fittings.

Standard: API 598 / BS 6755 Target: Valves, Pipes & Fittings
Test Range Class 150# - 2500# 1.5x Shell Holding Pressure
Metallurgical Audit

Spectrometry & MTC Heat Verification

Direct Positive Material Identification (PMI) using optical emission spectrometry. Verifies raw material heat numbers and alloy percentages (Cr, Ni, Mo, Ti) against EN 10204 3.1 Material Test Certificates (MTC).

Standard: ASTM A106 / SS316L / Inconel Scope: All Raw Castings & Billets
Certification EN 10204 3.1 100% Heat Number Matched
Non-Destructive Testing

Ultrasonic (UT) & X-Ray Radiography (RT)

Detects internal subsurface flaws, inclusions, porosity, and wall thickness variations inside heavy castings, thick-wall seamless pipes, and multi-axis CNC components without damaging the workpiece.

Standard: ASME Section V Target: Heavy Castings & Seamless Pipe Welds
Flaw Detection Sub-Surface Level Volumetric Internal Scans
Mechanical Testing

Brinell / Rockwell Hardness & Hydrogen Bake Check

Measures surface hardness and verifies heat treatment cycles (annealing, hardening, tempering). Includes hydrogen embrittlement relief baking audits for electroplated or galvanized high-tensile fasteners.

Standard: NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 Target: Sour Gas & High-Stress Fasteners
Hardness Standard HBW / HRC Sour Service Compliant
Dimensional Audit

CMM Blueprint & Precision Geometry Review

High-precision Coordinate Measuring Machine (CMM) dimensional verification against engineering CAD drawings. Ensures exact flange pitch circle diameter (PCD), thread pitch, bore concentricity, and ±0.005mm tolerances.

Tolerance: Up to ±0.005 mm Target: Custom CNC & Machined Parts
CMM Precision ±0.005 mm Laser Coordinate Verification
Stage-Gate Workflow

How We Prevent Production Delays

By catching defects early at the SME shop floor level, we eliminate re-work cycles and ensure on-time delivery.

GATE 01 // RAW MATERIAL

Chemical & Spectro Audit

No casting billet or pipe length enters the SME machining line until its spectro chemical composition matches heat numbers.

GATE 02 // IN-PROCESS

First-Piece & Tooling Inspection

CNC tool offsets and initial machined samples are verified against CMM parameters before starting full batch production.

GATE 03 // DISPATCH

Hydro-Test & MTC Sign-Off

Final pressure testing is logged, third-party inspection sign-offs are compiled, and complete MTC dossiers accompany shipment.
